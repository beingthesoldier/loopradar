About

What it is
kristudio turns a beat into a short vertical video that loops. Each preset is its own visual system; its colours and details are settings inside it.
How it works
The studio listens to your track: it finds the kick and the 808, the melody and the hats, and the pitch of each note, and the preset draws every hit. You pick the section, any number of bars. Everything moves in whole cycles of that section, so the video loops without a seam.
Your beat
The track is analysed and the video is made right in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
Export
MP4 (H.264 and AAC), 9:16, 720p or 1080p, 30 or 60 fps. The section once, or repeated up to three times. That's the format Reels, TikTok and Shorts take.
Browser
A recent Chrome or Safari on a computer.
